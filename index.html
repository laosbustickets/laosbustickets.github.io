Southeast Asia Transport

Getting around six countries of Southeast Asia, by train, bus, boat and plane

Six independent travel guides, one per country, explain where to board, how long each trip takes, what it costs and how to cross the borders. Each guide is written in eight languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Traditional Chinese.

The six guides

Laos

Laos Bus Tickets

Buses across Laos, the Laos–China Railway with a timetable read every morning, and every land border with Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia and China.

laosbustickets.com
Thailand

Thailand Transport Hub

Thai trains, long-distance buses, the Gulf and Andaman ferries, Bangkok's two airports and the crossings into Laos and Cambodia.

thailandtransporthub.com
Vietnam

Vietnam Transport Guide

The north–south railway, sleeper buses, domestic flights and ferries, with the exact station or office for each trip and the land borders.

vietnamtransportguide.com
Cambodia

Cambodia Transport Guide

Buses, the train, ferries to the islands and tuk-tuks, plus the border crossings, the e-visa and the e-Arrival card.

cambodiatransportguide.com
Philippines

Philippines Transport

Inter-island ferries and fast craft, buses and domestic flights, port by port, from Batangas and Cebu to Bohol, Palawan and Siargao.

philippinestransport.com
Indonesia

Indonesia Transport

Fast boats from Bali to Nusa Penida, the Gili Islands and Lombok, Java's trains, the car ferries and short domestic flights.

indonesiatransport.com

The open data behind the guides

What the guides check for travellers is also published as open data, so anyone can reuse it in an app, a map or research:

  • border crossings with their opening hours, visa status on each side and sources
  • bus, train, ferry and flight routes, and the inter-island ferry network
  • the Laos–China Railway timetable for the next seven days, also as a GTFS feed
  • each country's transport map as GeoJSON, and as images in eight languages

Files come as CSV, JSON and GeoJSON. Every record carries the date it was last checked. The data is free to use with attribution: CC BY 4.0, and ODbL 1.0 for files that contain OpenStreetMap coordinates.

Which copy to trust

Each website is the original source. The datasets are copies refreshed from the websites; if a copy and its website ever differ, the website is right, and its pages are the ones to cite.
